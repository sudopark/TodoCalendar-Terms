# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-10-22**

To-do Calendar ("the Service") is developed and operated by Hyunsoo Park ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Service handles, why, and what choices you have.

If you have questions, contact us at **todocalendar.help@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Data we collect

You can use the core features of the Service offline without an account. Signing in enables synchronization across devices and AI features.

### 1.1 Account

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| User identifier, email address | Identifying your account and syncing your data |
| Sign-in provider (Google or Apple) | Authentication |
| First and most recent sign-in time | Account management and abuse prevention |

Authentication tokens are stored in the Keychain on your device. They are shared, through an app group, with the app's own widgets, share extension and Siri shortcut extensions so that those can display and update your data; those extensions talk to our servers using the same token.

### 1.2 Content you create

When you are signed in, the content you create is stored on our servers so it can sync across your devices:

- To-dos and schedules — title, time, repeat rule, notification settings, tag
- Tags — name and colour
- Event details — **place name, coordinates (latitude and longitude), address**, link URL, and notes

Coordinates are stored only when you attach a place to an event. We do not collect background or continuous location data, and the Service does not request location permission.

### 1.3 AI features

When you use an AI command, the **text of that command** and your time zone are sent to our servers for processing.

- **Voice input** is transcribed by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio itself is never sent to our servers — only the resulting text.
- **Image input** is read by Apple's on-device text recognition. The image itself is never sent to our servers — only the recognised text.

**To interpret your command, our servers pass it to our AI model provider, Anthropic (Claude API).** While carrying out the command, the model may request your existing data, and **the to-dos and schedules it requests — including titles, times, notes, places and tags — are sent to Anthropic as well.** Anthropic handles this under its own policy and does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.

If you do not use AI features, none of this is sent.

The record of an AI command (the command text, the result and the token usage) is stored on our servers and **is deleted automatically after 24 hours.** We separately record daily usage totals in order to apply the usage limit of your plan; those totals do not contain the content of your commands.

### 1.4 Payments

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or receive your payment card details.

To activate what you bought, the Service sends Apple's **signed transaction record** and an app-generated purchase identifier to our servers, and stores your resulting plan status and remaining credits.

### 1.5 Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token and your device model so that we can deliver reminders to the right device.

### 1.6 Support enquiries

When you contact us through the app, we receive the message, your email address, your user identifier, and your app version, operating system version and device model, so that we can reproduce and answer the problem.

**Enquiries are delivered to Slack, the messaging tool we use to handle them. They do not pass through our own servers.**

### 1.7 Diagnostics

The Service includes Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, which collect usage statistics and crash reports. These may include device and operating-system information, an app instance identifier, and the state of the app at the time of a crash. **We do not send any custom events of our own — only the SDKs' standard collection (app opens, screen views) and crash reports.** We use this only to find and fix defects and to understand which features are used.

### 1.8 Advertising

On the free plan, the Service shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To select and display ads, AdMob processes device and operating-system information, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and records of ad impressions and clicks.

The Service includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure advertising performance. The SDK sends Meta app install and app open events, in-app purchase events (product, amount, currency), device and operating-system information, and an anonymous identifier generated by the SDK. Meta uses this to measure whether ads led to installs and purchases, and to optimise ad delivery.

**Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is passed to AdMob and Meta only if you allow the app's tracking request.** If you do not, the advertising identifier is not passed on.

This data is processed whether or not you are signed in. Content you create, such as to-dos and schedules, and your Google or Apple calendar data are never used for advertising.

## 2. Data that stays on your device

If you connect a Google or Apple calendar, the Service reads events from that calendar — including **titles, descriptions, locations, organisers, attendees (names, email addresses and response status), meeting links and attachments**.

**This data is stored only in the app's local database on your device. It is never sent to our servers.** It is removed when you disconnect the calendar account or delete the app.

Attendee details belong to other people. Please keep this in mind when sharing your device.

---

## 3. Google user data

The Service requests access to your Google Calendar in order to display your events and, where you choose to, to edit or delete them from within the app.

**Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.**

Concretely:

- Google Calendar data is used only to provide the calendar features visible to you in the app.
- It is stored only on your device (see section 2) and is not transferred to our servers.
- It is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties except where you explicitly ask us to, or where the law requires it.
- It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train generalised artificial-intelligence models.

You can revoke this access at any time by disconnecting the account in the app's settings, or from your [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 4. Device permissions

|---|---|
| Calendar (full access) | To read and write events in your Apple Calendar |
| Microphone | To capture voice input for creating events |

Each permission is requested only at the point you first use the corresponding feature, and the Service continues to work without it.

If you attach a web link to an event, **your device requests that page directly** in order to show a preview. The operator of that site may therefore see your IP address and the fact that the page was requested. This request does not pass through our servers.

## 6. Sharing

We do not sell your data.

We disclose data only:

- to the service providers listed above, to the extent needed to operate the Service;
- to Google AdMob and Meta, the data described in section 1.8, to show ads and measure advertising performance;
- when you ask us to; or
- when we are required to by law.

### Connected external apps

You can grant an external app — for example, an AI assistant connecting to the Service as a tool — access to your to-dos and schedules. A connection is only ever made when you approve it yourself on a consent screen.

- A connected app operates **only within the scope you approved.** There are two scopes, read (`read:calendar`) and write (`write:calendar`), and the consent screen shows which are being requested.
- We store the tokens issued for that connection, the details of the connected app, and the scopes you approved. Access tokens expire after 2 hours and refresh tokens after 30 days at most.
- **Data passed to a connected app goes to that app's operator, and what happens to it afterwards is governed by that app's privacy policy.** That part is outside our control.
- You can end a connection from the app you connected; doing so invalidates the issued tokens immediately. If that app offers no way to do so, contact us at the address below.

## 7. International transfers

The to-dos, schedules and other content you store are held in a Google Cloud database located in the **Republic of Korea (Seoul) region**.

The following processing, however, takes place outside that country:

| Recipient | Country | What is transferred | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud / Firebase | United States | Account data and to-do/schedule data as it passes through API requests, sign-in data, push tokens, diagnostics | Running our server functions, authentication, delivering notifications, diagnosing errors |
| Anthropic | United States | AI command text, and the to-do/schedule data needed to carry out the command | Interpreting and carrying out AI commands |
| Apple | United States | Purchase transaction records | Processing and verifying purchases |
| Slack | United States | Enquiry text, email address, user identifier, app and device information | Receiving and answering support enquiries |
| Google AdMob | United States | The advertising data described in section 1.8 | Showing ads |
| Meta | United States | The app events, purchase events and device information described in section 1.8 | Measuring advertising performance |

Transfers happen over the network at the moment you use the corresponding feature, and retention follows section 8. We rely on the safeguards those providers offer for such transfers.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

Records of AI commands are deleted automatically after 24 hours (see section 1.3).

You can delete your account from within the app at any time. Doing so requests deletion of your server-side data and removes your authentication record. We retain data beyond this point only where the law requires it.

Deleting the app without deleting your account removes the local database from your device but leaves your account data on our servers.

## 9. Children

The Service is a general-purpose productivity tool. It is not directed at children and contains no content aimed at children.

**We do not collect or verify age information.** We therefore cannot determine whether a given user is a child.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child's personal data has been provided to us, please contact **todocalendar.help@gmail.com**. We will verify the request and delete the data.

## 10. Security

Authentication credentials are stored in the device Keychain. Communication with our servers uses encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Your choices

- **Use without an account** — the core calendar and to-do features work offline, and the content you create is not sent to our servers. Diagnostics (section 1.7) and advertising data (section 1.8) are still sent without an account.
- **Refuse tracking** — decline the app's tracking request, or turn it off in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you do, the advertising identifier is not passed on.
- **Disconnect an external calendar** — at any time, in the app's settings.
- **Turn off notifications** — in your device settings.
- **Disconnect an external app** — from the app you connected; access ends immediately.
- **Delete your account** — in the app, which also requests deletion of your server-side data.
- **Ask us anything about your data** — at the contact address below.
