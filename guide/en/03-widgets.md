# 3. Widgets and Lock Screen

[← Contents](./README.md)

---

The point of a calendar widget is not having to open the calendar. To-do Calendar ships a wide set so you can pick the one that matches how you actually check your day — a single next event, a full month, or your to-do list with checkboxes you can tap.

---

## Home Screen widgets

### Today and What's Next

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sudopark/TodoCalendar-Terms/main/guide/images/widget-today-and-next.png" alt="Today and What's Next widget" width="360">

Today's date and what's left of today on the left, what's coming next on the right. The densest single view of "what now, what after".

*Medium.*

### Events

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sudopark/TodoCalendar-Terms/main/guide/images/widget-event-list.png" alt="Event list widget" width="300">

A running list of upcoming events grouped by day, including your current to-dos. Comes in three sizes, showing more days as it gets bigger.

*Small · Medium · Large.*

### Today

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sudopark/TodoCalendar-Terms/main/guide/images/widget-today.png" alt="Today widget" width="200">

Just today — the date, the holiday if there is one, and how many to-dos and schedules you have.

*Small.*

### Foremost Event

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sudopark/TodoCalendar-Terms/main/guide/images/widget-foremost.png" alt="Foremost event widget" width="200">

The one event you pinned as most important, always in view. See [Foremost event](./01-basics.md#foremost-event).

*Lock Screen inline · Small · Medium.*

### Month and weeks

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sudopark/TodoCalendar-Terms/main/guide/images/widget-month.png" alt="Month widget" width="360">

The calendar grid itself, in the range you want:

| Widget | Size |
|---|---|
| Calendar | Small |
| This week · 2 weeks | Medium |
| 3 weeks · 4 weeks | Large |
| Last month · This month · Next month | Large |

### Combinations

Two panels in one widget, when a single view isn't enough:

| Widget | Shows | Size |
|---|---|---|
| TODAY + Calendar | Today's summary next to the month grid | Medium |
| Events + Calendar | Upcoming events next to the month grid | Medium |
| Events + Foremost | Upcoming events next to your pinned event | Medium |
| Calendar + Calendar | Two months side by side | Medium |

### Add with AI

One tap straight into [AI quick input](./02-ai-input.md).

*Lock Screen circular · Small.*

## What you can do from a widget

- **Check a to-do off** — tap the circle on any to-do in a widget and it completes, without opening the app.
- **Tap through to the event** — tapping an event opens it straight to its detail screen.
- **Filter by event type** — long-press a widget, choose Edit Widget, and limit it to specific event types. Your own types and connected external calendars both show up in the picker.

---

## Lock Screen

### Lock Screen widgets

Several widgets have Lock Screen forms: **Next event** (inline and rectangular), **Today's next events** (rectangular), **Foremost Event** (inline), and **Add with AI** (circular).

### Live Activity countdown

Put an event on your Lock Screen and watch the time to it count down, with the same view in the Dynamic Island. Choose **Show on Lock Screen** from an event's more menu.

- Available for events starting within the next 8 hours.
- One event at a time — picking a new one asks whether to replace the current one.
- To-dos can be completed straight from the Live Activity.

---

## Control Center

On iOS 18 and later you can add the **Add with AI** control to Control Center, so a swipe down and one tap gets you to the AI input screen from anywhere.

---

## Appearance

Widgets follow your system light/dark setting by default, or you can pin them to a background color of your choice — the app picks readable text automatically based on how bright that color is. Set it in **Settings › Appearance › Widget theme**. See [Personalization](./05-personalization.md).

Widgets refresh on their own through the day and update right after you change something in the app.
